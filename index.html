🔋 Battery Health Tips
  • ⚡ Avoid frequent fast charging (DC). Use AC charging (7-22kW) for daily charging to preserve battery health.
  • 📊 Keep battery between 20-80% for daily use. Only charge to 100% for long trips.
  • 🌡️ Avoid charging when battery is hot. Let it cool down 30 mins after driving before charging.
  • 🔌 Don't leave car plugged in at 100% for extended periods. Unplug when charging completes.
  • 📅 If storing car for >1 week, leave battery at 50-60% charge level.
  • 🚗 Precondition battery while plugged in before driving in extreme temperatures.
⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This calculator provides estimates based on manufacturer specifications and typical charging efficiency data. Actual results may vary due to:

  • Battery temperature and ambient conditions (hot/cold weather increases losses)
  • Battery age and health degradation over time
  • Charging cable quality and length
  • Actual charger efficiency varying by brand/model
  • Charging above 80% is less efficient due to cell balancing
  • AC charging: ~10% loss | DC charging: ~7% loss (typical)

Use this calculator as a reference guide only. Always monitor your actual charging sessions for accurate data.
